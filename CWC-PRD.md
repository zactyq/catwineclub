CWC Mega App
Problem Statement
Cat Wine Club runs on group chats. Deals get posted into a thread and scroll away before half the club has seen them, event sign-ups are counted by hand, and nobody has a shared view of who has claimed what. As the club grows, allocations get missed or double-counted and the admins spend their evenings on admin instead of on wine.
The CWC app gives the community one place to see what is on offer, claim an allocation and sign up for events. It is an organising tool, not a shop. Payment, collection and delivery stay exactly as they are today, arranged directly between members and admins.
Personas
* The Organiser (admin) - sources the deals, posts the group buys and runs the events. Doing it for love, not money, so time is the scarce resource. Wants to list a batch of wines in one go and see live counts without chasing people in chat.
* The Regular (active member) - at most tastings, first to reply when a deal drops. Wants to know the moment something is listed and claim it in a couple of taps before it goes.
* The Dabbler (casual member) - in the club but not in the chat all day, so keeps missing things. Wants one page showing what is currently open, without scrolling back through hundreds of messages.
* The Curious (prospective member) - heard about CWC from a friend. Wants to understand what the club is, what is expected of members, and how to apply.


Group Buy Feature
Cat Wine Club App will have a group buy feature so that all our members can share their good deals with each other and take advantage of bulk discounts and combined deliveries.
Key User Stories
1. As an admin of CWC, I want to input details of wines for group buy such as Original Price, Discounted , Wine Name, Wine Vintage, Wine Description, Image, Quantity Available, so that I can offer these wines to the CWC members on a first come first served basis.
2. As an admin of CWC, I want to be able to input the details of multiple groupbuy wines in JSON format, so that I can use AI to help me speed up the process of inputting data into the database.
3. As a CWC member, I want to be able to post wine deals for group buy such as Original Price, Discounted , Wine Name, Wine Vintage, Wine Description, Image, Quantity Available, so that I can offer some of my wines to the CWC members on a first come first served basis.
4. As a CWC member, I want to browse through the available group buy wines, so that I can make a reservation for the allocation of the group buy wines.


Event Sign Up Feature
This feature allows admins to create and manage club events, while members can browse upcoming events and sign up for participation in real-time.
Key User Stories
1. As an admin of CWC, I want to input the details of an upcoming event, such as event date, description, so that I can put up events with realtime availability for members to sign up for these events from the main page on a first come first served basis.
2. As an admin of CWC, I want to be able to input the details of multiple events in JSON format, so that I can use AI to help me speed up the process of inputting event data into the database.
3. As a CWC member, I want to browse through the available events, so that I can sign up for myself and my plus ones.


Home Page
The central landing page where visitors can learn about the club's guidelines and easily navigate to submit their membership applications.
Key User Stories
1. As a site visitor, I want to view the rules and guidelines of the club, so that I know what the club is all about.
2. As a site visitor, I want to submit my application to sign up for the club.




Membership Sign up
A streamlined registration process for new applicants to join the club, including an administrative dashboard for managing pending applications.
Key User Stories
1. As a site visitor, I want to complete a registration form with my details, so that I can apply for club membership.
2. As an admin, I want to view and manage pending membership applications, so that I can approve new members.
Wine Sales Agent
An intelligent assistant that provides personalized wine recommendations and real-time information on stock and product details to help members make informed decisions.
Key User Stories
1. As a member, I want to ask the Wine Sales Agent for recommendations based on my preferences, so that I can find wines I'll enjoy.
2. As a member, I want to query the Wine Sales Agent about stock availability or wine details, so that I can make quick decisions.




Rules and Guidelines
Welcome to the Cat Wine Club (CWC)!
We are a casual, not-for-profit, and non-registered community built purely for wine lovers. We do our absolute best to create an inclusive environment where new and experienced wine nerds feel comfortable and welcome!


1. Good Vibes & Event Conduct
* Respect the Hosts: While the Exco manages the club channels, our amazing event hosts put in the hard work to set up and dictate the rules for their specific events, Remember we are a wine appreciation club for all, do respect each other, keep everyone comfortable and be as inclusive as possible.
* Pace Yourself: We want everyone to have a great time, so please pace yourself! Try not to get too drunk, carted out, or sick. Dealing with messes or overly drunk behavior makes the community uncomfortable and divided, so let's all look out for one another. And for the party animals, there’s always space for you guys to go wild at your own “round twos” after the event proper.
* Buddy System: If we see our friends getting a bit too rowdy, let's help them get home ASAP. Not only does this keep them safe, but it also maintains a respectable environment for everyone else who still wants to have good, clean fun!
* Accountability & "OOPS" Fees: Accidents happen! But if you make a mess, please clean it up. If you break a glass, there is an ‘OOPS’ fee, please offer to pay for it, as they do not come free. Most importantly, if feelings get hurt or anyone is upset, please make peace and patch things up before the next event.
* BYOB Etiquette: For BYOB days/events, please try your best to stay on theme and avoid bringing petrol station wine. If you need help finding good bottles, reach out to Isaac or Luke! We’re super friendly and can point you to the right distributors for good prices, or even share some gems from our personal cellars at a fair market rate.
2. The "Yellow Card" System 
* The Warning: If you get into trouble or cause a bit too much mischief, the event hosts have full authority to hand out a ‘yellow card’.
* Atonement: You can clear your name by being proactive and asking for forgiveness. Also, feel free to contribute to the CWC fund (which usually goes toward Champagne or your welcome drinks!) OR do some community service by organizing an upcoming event... OR simply buy a lovely bottle of Montrachet for the next gathering!
* Strike Two: Two yellow cards mean a red card. In that case, we’ll gently have to ask you to sit out of events for your own good until you've recovered and are ready to rejoin us after rehab.


3. Invites, Promos, & Socials
* Growing the Community: Feel free to invite friends by asking them to the next event and signing up on their behalf! We’ll ask them nicely in person if they’d like to join the chat group. (Always ask for their consent first, please!)
* Posting & Promos: Have a 3rd party event, promotional content, group buy, or just want to jio people out for a drink? Feel free to post it in a respectful manner.
* Tag Us: Please help post and tag @catwineclub on IG… our social media game kinda sucks right now and we'd love the help!


4. RSVPs & Group Housekeeping
* Cancellations: If you can’t make it to a paid event, please give a heads-up and try your absolute best to find a replacement.
* Lurkers: If you're quietly lurking and find our events aren't your glass of wine, feel free to leave the group—zero pressure or hard feelings! Otherwise, just a heads-up that we maaay do some light housekeeping yearly on our anniversary to clear out inactive folks who haven't attended all year. We just want to keep the chat active and alive!


5. Get Involved!
* If you have any great ideas, want to run a wine event with us, or are interested in joining the Exco, please reach out to any of the Exco team (for now, that’s Luke, Charis, and Isaac).
* We’ll help you get set up wherever possible, and then we’ll have a fun, not-so-secret internal vote to decide the fate of your event!
